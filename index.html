Behind the mic

Lydia (left) is a primary school teacher in Switzerland. Ami (right) has her master's in education and worked as a high school teacher for many years.

Working in education, we noticed how many things are never taught and yet still expected of us. We started the podcast to help combat this — breaking down simple, everyday things you're expected to know as an adult but were never actually taught.

About the show

Ever wondered how to budget, do laundry, or study for an exam? Each week, we break down everyday life skills and make them simple, entertaining, and easy to understand.
